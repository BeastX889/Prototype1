Round Timer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Short version: Round Timer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Round Timer has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your timer settings, custom presets, and workout history locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app (or when you use “Clear history” in the app).

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to ring round bells while the app is in the background. Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — lets those background bells ring at the exact second a round ends instead of being delayed by battery optimization. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback at round transitions.

The app does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or storage access.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync), the updated policy will be posted at this address before the change takes effect.
